Bluesky stopped selling domains. What happens to my handle?

For people whose Bluesky handle is a domain they bought through Bluesky's own domain service (account.bsky.app, run with Namecheap since 2023). Written 11 November 2026; I'll update it when Bluesky publishes next steps.

What the notice says

As of 11 November 2026, account.bsky.app shows this (copied word for word):

New domain purchases are closed. Bluesky is no longer accepting new domain registrations, inbound transfers, or renewal payments through this service.

Already have a domain? Your existing domain registration remains in place. You do not need to take action right now. Bluesky will share next steps once they are finalized.

Made a recent payment? Bluesky is closing this service and will reach out to customers to transfer their domains. Contact support if you need help now.

This isn't brand new. The page showed renewals as "paused" first; by 25 September 2026 people were posting that it had switched to "closed", and on 28 September users were already quoting the "will reach out to customers to transfer their domains" line above. So the notice has been up for about six weeks, and as of 11 November no next steps have appeared on it. I couldn't find a blog post or an announcement beyond this page, so the details below are what is known for sure, not guesses about Bluesky's plans.

What happens to your account if the domain lapses

  • Your account, posts and followers are safe. They belong to your account's permanent ID (its DID), not to the domain. Only the handle, the name people see, depends on the domain.
  • The handle shows "Invalid Handle" once Bluesky re-checks and finds the domain gone. You can still log in with your email address and password.
  • Old mentions still point to you, because posts store the account ID, not the name.
  • Links that use the name may not. Addresses like bsky.app/profile/yourdomain.com and business cards rely on the domain. If someone else registers it later, they could use the name for their own account.

What you can check now

  • Does your handle still work? Run it through the Bluesky handle checker. It looks up the record the way Bluesky does and says if the domain has expired, been parked, or lost its record.
  • When does the domain expire? ICANN's lookup tool (lookup.icann.org) shows the registrar and an expiry date. Domains bought through Bluesky list NameCheap, Inc. as the registrar. Careful: for many endings (.com, .net, .gay and others) the registry moves the date a year forward on the day the domain expires, before anyone has paid. On 11 November 2026 I saw two Bluesky-bought domains whose date had jumped to 2027 that same day. A date in the future does not prove the domain is paid for. Your renewal emails from Bluesky are the better guide to when it was really due.
  • Is your old .bsky.social name still yours? Bluesky's domain handle guide says that since an update on 12 December 2024, your most recent .bsky.social name is reserved for you when you switch to a domain, and the reservation doesn't expire. It doesn't say whether that covers switches made before that date, so if you switched earlier, open bsky.app/profile/yourname.bsky.social and see if anyone else has it.

What you can do

  • If the domain is due soon, or already expired: contact Bluesky support now rather than waiting, as the notice suggests (the in-app support form, or support@bsky.app). Mention the domain and when it was due.
  • If it isn't due for months: the notice says you don't need to do anything yet. Keep an eye on your email for Bluesky's transfer instructions.
  • To keep the same handle long-term, the domain has to end up registered somewhere you can renew it yourself. Bluesky says it will reach out about transfers. Once a domain is at another registrar, the handle keeps working as long as the _atproto TXT record moves with it; the checker gives you the exact value if you need to re-add it.
  • If you'd rather not keep the domain: change your handle back any time in the app: Settings → Account → Handle, type a name, Save. Your posts and followers come with you.
