Tools

  • Bluesky character counter: counts a post the way the Bluesky app does (graphemes, links shortened first, 300 limit) and shows the exact text that gets posted. Also warns when a quirk in the app's link shortening would garble your text.
  • Bluesky thread splitter: paste long text, get posts that each fit the 300 limit as the app counts it, broken at paragraphs and sentences, with optional 1/N numbering.
  • Bluesky handle checker: handle shows "Invalid Handle", or the domain you set up isn't working? Checks the _atproto TXT record and .well-known file the way Bluesky does, plus the account side, and says what to fix.
  • Bluesky stopped selling domains: what happens to my handle? If your handle is a domain you bought through Bluesky: what the closure notice says, what happens if the domain lapses, what to check now.
  • facet-check: build Bluesky rich-text facets with correct UTF-8 byte offsets, or paste a post to see why a link, mention or #tag landed on the wrong text. Runs in your browser. Same detection rules as the official @atproto/api library (checked on 18,354 random texts).
  • Why is my Bluesky link on the wrong text?: a short guide to byteStart/byteEnd, with JavaScript and Python snippets.
  • bsky-tiny: a one-file, standard-library-only Python Bluesky client (post with links/mentions/tags, images, replies). MIT.
